# SENTINEL AI

## Global Vehicle Quality & Predictive Risk Intelligence Platform

### ROLE

You are a senior staff-level software architect, AI engineer, product designer, data scientist, cybersecurity engineer, and enterprise SaaS developer.

Build a production-quality MVP called **Sentinel AI**.

Sentinel AI is an independent automotive technology concept designed to help vehicle manufacturers identify emerging quality, reliability, warranty, and potential recall risks before they become large-scale problems.

The application must look and behave like a serious enterprise AI product that could eventually be demonstrated to a global automotive manufacturer.

Do NOT build a simple dashboard.

Build a complete, polished, technically credible platform with realistic synthetic automotive data, explainable AI, predictive analytics, an AI investigation assistant, risk scoring, and an executive-quality user interface.

The system must clearly distinguish between:

1. Real/public information
2. Synthetic/demo data
3. AI-generated hypotheses

Never claim that synthetic data is actual manufacturer data.

---

# CORE PRODUCT VISION

Sentinel AI continuously analyzes multiple automotive-quality signals and identifies weak signals that may indicate an emerging vehicle problem.

The platform should answer:

* What is going wrong?
* Where is it happening?
* Which vehicles are affected?
* When did the signal begin?
* Which component or software version may be responsible?
* How confident is the system?
* What evidence supports the conclusion?
* What should engineers investigate?
* What could happen if the issue is ignored?
* How much potential exposure could exist?
* How early did Sentinel detect the issue?

The central product concept is:

> DETECT → INVESTIGATE → EXPLAIN → PRIORITIZE → ACT

Examples:

### Scenario A

A component batch has an unusually high failure rate after approximately 30,000 km.

### Scenario B

A software version correlates with increased failures of another subsystem.

### Scenario C

### Scenario D
